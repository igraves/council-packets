# Councilmember Inga Selders: Votes on the City Hall / Municipal Complex Project

*Covers council minutes from September 2022 through July 20, 2026. Companion to [pv-municipal-complex-history.md](pv-municipal-complex-history.md).*

Selders voted on 30 motions about the project, from the first Clark & Enersen contract to the 2026 construction contracts.

## How votes were determined

The minutes usually name only the dissenters, so most of Selders's votes are inferred rather than recorded by name. Each vote is labeled:

- **Recorded:** the minutes or the extracted vote record name her vote.
- **Inferred:** she is on that meeting's roll call and is not named among the dissenters, or the vote was unanimous. An abstention the minutes didn't record would be missed.
- **Absent:** she is not on that meeting's roll call.

Citations are `(YYYY-MM-DD minutes, p. N)`, using PDF page indices. Where no page is given, the vote is in that meeting's minutes and was found through the extracted vote record (`votes.json`).

## Attendance verification

Every inferred vote rests on Selders being present, so attendance was checked in two independent sources for each meeting:

1. **Standalone minutes:** the roll call in that meeting's minutes file.
2. **Approved minutes:** the same minutes as reprinted for approval in the next meeting's council packet.

Both sources agree for all 18 meetings. None of the minutes records Selders arriving after the roll call, leaving early, recusing herself, or abstaining on any of these votes. Late arrivals of other councilmembers *are* noted when they happen (Cole Robinson on 2024-11-18, Reddell on 2024-12-16 and 2025-05-05), so the minutes do track this.

## Related actions without a vote

- **2024-12-02:** Selders spoke in favor of the purchase (2024-12-02 minutes, p. 4). Sharp moved to put the project cost to a public ballot, then withdrew it before any vote (pp. 3–4).
- **2024-11-18:** Selders opposed changing the church's exterior and wanted more time on the options (2024-11-18 minutes, pp. 7–8).
- **2025-05-05:** Selders said she and Sharp had been blocked from putting church rehabilitation on an agenda. The Mayor cited the failed January 21 vote (2025-05-05 minutes, p. 3).

## The pattern

1. **She supported the existing-lot plan.** She voted for every step of the 2022–2024 plan to build on the existing lot, including seconding the contested 6-4 schematic design approval.
2. **She supported buying the church.** She seconded both the purchase contract and the acquisition resolution.
3. **She opposed demolishing the church for a new building.** She voted no on Option 2A (decided 6-5 on the Mayor's tie-break), moved to put demolition on the agenda, pushed to discuss rehabilitating the church, and voted against LEED Platinum.
4. **She was absent for both formal reconsideration points.** These were Sharp's January 2025 motion to study renovating the church and the June 2025 project and bond authorization.
5. **She voted to carry out the decision once it was final.** After the lawsuit was dismissed, she voted for the December 2025 bonds and the April 2026 guaranteed maximum price (GMP) and asbestos contracts.
