## 2027 budget (adopted)

Staff presented the 2027 budget as holding the mill levy flat at 18.057 mills (after a reduction the prior year), citing uncertainty from possible state property-tax legislation and the pending expiration of the county public safety sales tax (~$570,000 loss) as reasons not to cut the rate further. The General Fund 'all-in' total of $34,538,314 represents a 3.4% increase over 2026, driven mainly by continued implementation of the 2022 compensation study and 2024 police market adjustments, full CIP transfer funding, and debt service transfers for the Municipal Complex and Public Works Facility. The overall certified budget across all funds totals $52,081,648 in budget authority, with an ad valorem tax levy of $13,815,370, exceeding the revenue neutral rate of 16.973 mills, which required a public hearing and resolution before adoption on September 8, 2026.

## 2025 budget (adopted)

General Fund expenditures rose 1.3% to $32,005,383, driven mainly by a new police department pay scale (about $425,000, two-thirds of the total increase) and a 4% merit pool for other employees, alongside increases in contract services and commodities and a decrease in capital outlay. The budget also sets aside an additional $1,150,000 transfer to the Bond and Interest Fund for potential city hall improvements, while maintaining a General Fund reserve slightly above the 25% target at 25.1%. ## 2024 budget (adopted)

## 2018 budget (adopted)

The 2018 adopted budget for Prairie Village totals $45.3 million in all-fund expenditures, up from $35.9 million in 2017, driven largely by a near-doubling of Capital Infrastructure Fund spending for street, drainage and park projects and higher debt service following new G.O. bond issuance. The General Fund budget (including transfers) grew to about $22.9 million, supported by a higher property tax levy ($7.06 million, up from $6.33 million) and continued growth in sales, use and franchise tax revenues. Staff also restructured some costs, moving Information Technology from Administration to the Police Department, and increased transfers to the Equipment Reserve and Capital Infrastructure Funds to address equipment and infrastructure needs.
